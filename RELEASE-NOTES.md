# Release notes

What changed in **One More Tile** for the people playing it, newest first.
[CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) has the full technical record of each version.

## [0.33.1] 2026-09-25: Versus and menu fixes

- In online versus, a joining player now sees the host on the scoreboard and
  in the opponents panel.
- A guest who wins is told they won, not "Player 3 wins".
- You can host your most recent canvas online straight from its Continue card.
- The join panel says you joined once the board has arrived.

## [0.33.0] 2026-09-25: Safer online play

- In a game with two or more guests, one guest could pretend to be the host:
  push a board at the others, fake a match result or disconnect them. That's
  no longer possible.
- Joining a friend's game no longer makes your own solo progress on that
  canvas look empty.
- Nobody can fill a room with fake players to keep your friends out.
- During a versus match, nobody can stitch on your board or replace it, and
  sudoku digits only count between "Go!" and the final whistle.
- The online library now ships with the game instead of loading from another
  site, and online play works when you open the game file straight off disk.
- The versus lobby says plainly that each player reports their own score.

## [0.32.0] 2026-09-25: Versus mode

- Race up to three friends for the canvas, on one screen or online with a code.
- On one screen you share the canvas, and each region goes to whoever
  stitched most of it. A nearly finished region can still be stolen.
- Online, everyone solves their own copy of the same puzzle. You see your
  rivals' progress and a minimap of where they're working, never their colours.
- Three rules: play out the full canvas, first to a number of regions, or
  most regions when the clock stops.
- A match never touches your saved progress.
- Fixed: sudoku digits placed with a mouse or controller now count for the
  player who placed them, touch stitches in split-screen go to the right
  player, and players 2 to 4 can undo their own moves.

## [0.31.0] 2026-09-25: A brand-new puzzle from any canvas

- "New weave" in the menu gives any canvas a completely different puzzle:
  same painting and regions, new solution and clues.
- Each weave keeps its own save, so you can go back to the original and pick
  up where you left off.
- Share codes and co-op carry the weave, so friends get the same puzzle.

## [0.30.0] 2026-09-25: No more waiting to start

- Every canvas ships ready-made. Opening one used to take up to 35 seconds
  the first time. Now it's the length of the loading animation.
- Every player gets exactly the same board on every browser.

## [0.29.0] 2026-08-10: See your progress on the canvas cards

- Map cards show the regions you've finished in full colour, plus a progress

- Resetting a canvas clears its card too.
